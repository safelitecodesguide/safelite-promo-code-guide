How do you use a Safelite promo code?

  1. Go to safelite.com and start a quote for repair or replacement.
  2. Choose "Pay on my own" — codes do not apply to insurance claims.
  3. On the quote screen, open "I have a promo code" and enter the code.
  4. Check the discount line appears before you book. If it doesn't, try the next code from the list above.

Where does this data come from?

The code results on this site come from checkout testing published by SafelatesCoupons.us, which enters each code into a live safelite.com quote and records the exact discount — most recently on October 21, 2026. Safelite's own site is the other source: its coupons page lists WEBSL45 and WEB15SL, and its hero discount page publishes the two SAFELITEHERO codes. Read the testing method — and always confirm a code in your own checkout before booking, because results vary by vehicle, service and ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Safelite promo code right now?

ADDS100. It took $100 off a replacement quote in checkout testing on October 21, 2026 — the largest saving of the 14 codes that passed. For a chip repair, SAVENOW2026 is best: $25 off.

How many Safelite promo codes actually work?

14 codes applied at safelite.com checkout in the latest test. Five other widely listed codes (including SAVE20SL and AAA10SL) were rejected, so a long list on another site does not mean the codes work.

Can I use a Safelite promo code with insurance?

No. The promo field appears during the insurance flow, but Safelite's offer terms say codes are not valid on insurance claims. Codes apply when you pay on your own and schedule on safelite.com.

Can I stack two Safelite promo codes?

No — the checkout accepts one code per order. Pick the single biggest code for your job: ADDS100 for replacement, SAVENOW2026 for repair.

Do Safelite promo codes expire?

Safelite does not publish expiry dates for these codes, so this site never prints one. Codes do stop working without notice, which is why they are re-tested at checkout regularly — the date on every card is the last test date.

Is there a Safelite code for a $35 chip repair?

Safelite advertises a $35 chip-repair deal, but it did not apply automatically in checkout tests on October 17 or October 21, 2026. A repair code is the reliable route: SAVENOW2026 took $25 off a $164.99 repair quote.

Where do I enter a Safelite promo code?

During online scheduling at safelite.com, choose 'Pay on my own', get your quote, and use the 'I have a promo code' link before booking. The full walkthrough is on our How to Use page.

Does Safelite give military or AAA discounts?

Yes — through one code: 10THANKYOU10. Safelite's FAQ names it as the 10% offer for military members, AAA, AARP and first responders. Eligibility is verified at your appointment, and it excludes recalibration, insurance claims and deductibles.

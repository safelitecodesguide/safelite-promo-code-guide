Why do dead codes stay listed everywhere?

Most coupon sites never test anything — they copy lists from each other and roll the dates forward. A code can stay "active" on dozens of sites for months after it stops working. The only proof that counts is the checkout itself, which is why this guide dates every test and publishes the failures alongside the successes.

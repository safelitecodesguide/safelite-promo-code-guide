What about the $35 chip-repair deal?

Safelite promotes a $35 chip-repair offer, but in checkout tests on October 17 and again on October 21, 2026, no $35 price applied automatically in either booking flow. Don't count on it appearing. A tested code is the dependable saving: SAVENOW2026 took the $164.99 repair quote to $139.99.

Repair or replace?

As a rule, chips smaller than a dollar bill and cracks away from the edge and the driver's line of sight can usually be repaired. Bigger damage means replacement — where the discounts are larger, up to $100 with ADDS100. See the replacement codes.

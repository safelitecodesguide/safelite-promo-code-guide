About this guide

This is an independent research guide to Safelite promo codes. It exists for one reason: most coupon pages list codes nobody has ever tried. This one starts from codes that were actually entered at Safelite's checkout, with the discount and the test date recorded — and it says so plainly when a famous code fails.

What this site is not

  • Not Safelite, and not affiliated with Safelite AutoGlass. To book, manage or warranty a job, go to safelite.com or call Safelite.
  • Not a guarantee. Codes change; the last test date is printed on every code, and you should confirm in your own quote.
  • Not a copy of other coupon sites. Expired or rejected codes are labelled as such, never dressed up as deals.

Safelite discounts beyond promo codes

Groups: military members, AAA and AARP members and first responders are named in Safelite's own FAQ for the 10THANKYOU10 offer (eligibility checked at the appointment; recalibration, insurance claims and deductibles excluded). Insurance: with comprehensive cover, a repair may cost you nothing out of pocket and a replacement only your deductible — often a better deal than any code, which is why the code list is aimed at people paying themselves. In the quote itself: Safelite sometimes shows package savings (a "Save $40" premium special appeared in testing) that apply before any code.

What is the best Safelite promo code?

ADDS100 — and it isn't close. In checkout testing on October 21, 2026, it took $100 off a windshield replacement quote ($787.63 → $687.63), the largest saving of any code tested. The same code also passed in all 10 ZIP codes in an earlier multi-ZIP check.

Best code for repair

SAVENOW2026 ($25 off) beats the repair-specific codes: SAFELITEHEROREPAIR20 ($20), then PFF15 and WEB15SL ($15 each). On the $164.99 sample repair quote, SAVENOW2026 brought it to $139.99.

Why not just use the percentage code?

10THANKYOU10 sounds like the winner — "10% off" — but in two separate checkout tests it reduced the quote by about 4–5% of the total ($39.08 off $787.63; $45.62 off $1,214.27), because it applies to part of the bill, not the whole quote. The flat $100 from ADDS100 beat it on both vehicles tested. If your quote is very large, enter both codes one at a time and keep whichever discount is bigger — checkout only keeps one.

Honest limit: these rankings come from real quotes on specific vehicles in ZIP 30303. A code can behave differently on another vehicle or in another area — which is why every card on this site shows its last test date, and why you should confirm the discount line in your own quote before booking.

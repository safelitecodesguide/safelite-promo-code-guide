Best code for repair

Three codes tie at $25 off: SAVENOW2026, MIL25 and SOCIALREP25. They beat the repair-specific codes: SAFELITEHEROREPAIR20 ($20), then PFF15, WEB15SL and 15RPRCJ ($15 each), and CJRMNRPR10 ($10). On the $164.99 sample repair quote, each $25 code brought it to $139.99.

Why not just use the percentage code?

10THANKYOU10 sounds like the winner — "10% off" — but in checkout tests it reduced the quote by only part of that headline rate ($35.25 off $749.38 in the latest test; $45.62 off $1,214.27 on an October 17, 2026 sample), because it applies to part of the bill, not the whole quote. The flat $100 from ADDS100 or LCQ426RCL beat it on both vehicles tested. If your quote is very large, enter the codes one at a time and keep whichever discount is bigger — checkout only keeps one.

Honest limit: these rankings come from real quotes on specific vehicles in ZIP 30303. A code can behave differently on another vehicle or in another area — which is why every card on this site shows its last test date, and why you should confirm the discount line in your own quote before booking.

Repair cost

A one-chip repair quote was $164.99 (glass tier). With SAVENOW2026, MIL25 or SOCIALREP25 it fell to $139.99. Package tiers (standard/premium glass options) raise the base price above the glass-tier figures shown here.

What changes the price?

  • Vehicle and glass type — larger, newer and luxury vehicles cost more.
  • Camera/sensor recalibration after replacement on cars with driver-assist systems.
  • Mobile service versus in-shop, and your ZIP code.
  • Insurance versus paying yourself — with comprehensive cover you may only owe a deductible, but then promo codes don't apply.
